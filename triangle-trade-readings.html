The Triangle Trade

Choose the reading you want. You can switch at any time.

They all teach the same thing. Every level covers all five regions — New England, the Middle Colonies, the Southern Colonies, the West Indies and England — and every level teaches the same words for each region, so your team can talk about the same ideas no matter which one you picked. The longer ones say more about why things happened — they are not about being smarter.

Pick the one you can read comfortably. If it feels too hard or too easy, switch. There is a level switcher at the top of every reading.

Read your own region. Your team is one region in the game. Read your region on your own, then fill in your Region Notes.
