Escape Rooms

Group puzzles played in class, one lesson at a time

How they work: your group gets numbered envelopes of puzzles. Each puzzle gives a code, and you type it into the room’s lock page on one Chromebook. A wrong code freezes the lock for a minute, and every lock has two hints. Open the room Mr. B assigns in class.
