Unit 4: A New Nation & Its Economy

Weeks 20–23 · 1789–1840s

What kind of nation — and economy — would the young United States become?
  • When you are the first to do a job, how do your choices become rules for everyone who comes after?
  • When a young nation chases profit and growth, what values does it trade away — and who pays the price?
  • How did early economic decisions shape who held power in America?
Signature project: “Founding an Economy” cabinet simulation — students take on advisor roles and argue the debt, the bank, and the tariff, then defend the decision they made.
