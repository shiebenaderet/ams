Unit 2: Revolution: Ideas & Voices

Weeks 6–8 · Oct 5–23 · 1763–1783

What made independence worth fighting for — and for whom was it left incomplete?
  • How did loyal British subjects turn into revolutionaries in barely a decade?
  • The Declaration says “all men are created equal” — who was actually included, and who was left waiting?
  • Whose account of the Revolution survived, and whose did we have to go looking for?
Signature project: Revolutionary voices podcast — teams research an overlooked participant in the Revolution and produce an episode telling that story from the sources.

Activities & Games

Causes of Unrest Stations activity analyzing the grievances that led to revolution. Revolutionary Voices Hear from the people who lived through the Revolution. Revolution Review Review the key ideas leading up to the Revolution.
