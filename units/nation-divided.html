Unit 6: A Nation Divided

Weeks 28–31 · 1850–1877

Why did the nation tear itself apart, and what did Reconstruction try to rebuild?
  • Can you explain the Civil War without following the money?
  • What do cotton, tariffs, and enslaved labor add that “North vs. South” leaves out?
  • What did Reconstruction promise, and what happened to those promises?
Signature project: Causation analysis — students build an evidence-based claim about why the war came, using economic, political, and personal sources together.
