Unit 7: The Early Republic

How did the young nation navigate its first challenges and define its identity?
  • What precedents did the early presidents set?
  • How did foreign and domestic conflicts test the new government?
  • When should a government limit freedom to protect the nation?

Key Project: The Big Decisions

The young nation faced hard choices with no playbook. Students deliberate three real decisions in small teams, argue every side, and then make an art piece – a political cartoon, campaign poster, trading cards, or an illustrated letter – showing where they stand.
  • A National Bank? Hamilton vs. Jefferson, and how much power the government should have over money.
  • Stay Neutral? Should the young United States stay out of the revolutions in France and Haiti?
  • Safety or Freedom? The Alien and Sedition Acts, and the limits of free speech.
