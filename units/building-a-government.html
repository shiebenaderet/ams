Unit 3: Building a Government

Weeks 9–19 · 1781–1815

How do you build a government that limits its own power — and who decides what the Constitution means?
  • Why did America’s first government fail — and what does that failure teach us about power?
  • How do checks and balances actually work when they are tested?
  • When the Constitution is unclear, who gets the final say?
Signature project: The Constitutional CBA — the state-required Civics assessment, run as a SCOTUS-style debate. Built in December, argued in January, wrapping up by the end of Semester 1.

Note: This unit carries the required Washington State Civics CBA.

Activities & Games

Constitution Review Review the key ideas and provisions of the Constitution. SCOTUS Debate Project Research and debate landmark Supreme Court cases.
