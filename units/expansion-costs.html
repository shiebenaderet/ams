Unit 5: Expansion & Its Costs

Weeks 24–27 · Feb 22–Mar 19 · 1801–1850

What drove the nation westward, and who paid the price?
  • Was Manifest Destiny a genuine belief, a justification for taking land, or both — and does the difference matter?
  • What does sovereignty mean, and what happened when the nation ignored it?
  • What can layered maps show us about expansion that a textbook paragraph cannot?
Signature project: Layered map investigation — students build a map in layers (land, people, treaties, removal) and use it to argue what expansion actually cost.
