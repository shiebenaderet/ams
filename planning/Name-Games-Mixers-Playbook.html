Week 1 Launch — the opening ten

Icebreakers, name games, and collaborative launches for the first days of school. Several come with ready-made materials (linked on each card — keep those files in this folder so links resolve). The suggested sequence below meshes with the Unit 1 Blueprint: launch week is just Wed–Thu (Sep 2–3), then a 4-day week.

Suggested launch sequence

Day 1Wed Sep 2Welcome + name-story opening circle (Blueprint Day 1) → Which Came First? showdown — high energy, zero reading, everyone moves → exit ticket: one hope for this class.
Day 2Thu Sep 3 · Fri offCo-construct norms / Classroom Constitution (Blueprint Day 2) → Routine Relay round 1 (entry + attention signal) → Classroom Scavenger Hunt in random teams — learns the room, the procedures, and four classmates' names in one shot.
Day 3Tue Sep 8First WALT + success criteria (Blueprint Day 3) → Beat the Record install (first class record on the board) → Time Machine worksheet, share one answer by name at tables.
Day 4Wed Sep 9Life Map project launch (the Blueprint's identity life-map, now with the planning handout) → Get-to-Know-You Jenga rotation 1 while you conference.
Day 5Thu Sep 10"Now & Then" #1 (Blueprint Day 4) → Summer 2026 Current Events Showdown — table teams, whiteboards, ends on "your headline of the summer" name round. Collect the Who Said It? survey.
Day 6Fri Sep 11First retrieval Do Now (norms + procedures) → Investigate the Teacher clue hunt in pairs → Week-1-style closing circle: glow & grow (Blueprint Day 5).
Day 7Mon Sep 14Timeline Challenge starter round (10–12 cards) in pods — first taste of arguing chronology with evidence → Life Map studio time. Two Truths: Content Edition and the full 30-card deck wait for review weeks.

Where these live in the week: none of these need new time. Beat the Record and Who Said It? are Do Now / circle-slot sized (3–8 min). Concentric Circles and Common Ground replace one weekly community circle a month. Hot Take Showdown is a Friday closer. The Census fires only when pods re-form, and the two slow-burns (Secret Constituent, Liar's Dossier) run in the background off a weekly 4-minute ritual. If the room's energy dips after a break, run Beat the Record first — it re-installs names and the attention signal in one shot.
